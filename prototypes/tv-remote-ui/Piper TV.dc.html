1 Piper — RPi TV launcher, first pass

Assumptions: 1080p TV at ~2.5m, 5-way D-pad + number keys, native app auto-started when the Pi becomes the active HDMI source. Services are native launches; websites open in the built-in browser where the remote switches to cursor mode. No real brand logos — colored monogram tiles stand in, and artwork areas are striped placeholders. Every screen below is drawn at 1920×1080 and shown at 50%.
